Controls

  • Red arrows: walk. You fire the way you face.
  • Orange: fire. In the shop, buy or upgrade.
  • Blue: swing, which shakes off every biting dino. In the shop, buy ammo.
  • Green: swap gun, or enter and leave the shop at its door.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Can I turn off the gore? Yes, in Settings on the title screen.
