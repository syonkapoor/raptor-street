Raptor Street Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Synergi Insights LLC

Raptor Street is a free game for iPhone. This policy covers the Raptor Street app.

We collect nothing

Raptor Street does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (cash, weapons, level, records, character and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding ads, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
